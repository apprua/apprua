# Code of Conduct

Apprua is made for people who are not developers. Many of them will be asking their first question in an open source project: make it a good first time.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Apprua to be free from harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, social and economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners. There are no stupid questions here.
- Disagree about ideas, never about people.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and give it in a way that helps.
- Own your mistakes and learn from them.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, personal or political attacks, sexual language or images.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission.
- Any behaviour that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Write to **conduct@apprua.com**. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, code or issues that break these rules, and may ban people from the project, temporarily or for good.
