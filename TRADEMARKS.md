# The Apprua name and logo

The code of Apprua is free software under the AGPL-3.0: you can use it, study it, change it and share it. **The license covers the code, not the brand.** The name "Apprua" and the lighthouse logo (the letter A with its lamp, also where it appears in the illustrations) identify the original project, so that people know what they are installing and who stands behind it. The AGPL gives no rights to use them as a brand (its section 7 allows this to be kept apart).

This page says what you can do with them. It is written in plain words on purpose; if in doubt, ask.

## You can, without asking

- Say that your work **uses**, **works with** or **is based on** Apprua ("Publish with Apprua", "A plugin for Apprua", "Based on Apprua").
- Use the name and logo to **talk about the project**: articles, videos, talks, school work, community pages.
- Run Apprua, unchanged or changed, **for yourself or inside your organisation**, and keep calling it Apprua there.
- Share the **unchanged** installers and releases published by this repository.

## You need written permission to

- Use "Apprua", or a name that could be confused with it (for example "Apprua Pro", "Aprua", "MyApprua"), as the name of **your own product, service, company or domain**.
- Offer a **changed version** of Apprua to the public, or a hosted service built on it, **under the Apprua name or logo**. Changed versions are welcome: give them a different name and logo, and you may say they are "based on Apprua".
- Put the logo on **merchandise**, or use the logo or the name in your own marketing as the sign of your product.
- Suggest that your product or service is **official**, **endorsed** or **certified** by the Apprua project.

## When you use them

- When the logo stands for Apprua, do not change it: no other colours, no stretching, no extra elements. The brand files are in `brand/`.
- Write the name as **Apprua** (capital A, the rest lowercase) in text.
- Make clear that your project is yours, not the Apprua project's.

## The pictures inside the program

Two different rights are at play, and they do not mix:

- **Copyright.** The illustrations (the scenes of the portal in `packages/server/assets/img`) and the logo and wordmark files in `brand/` are part of this repository and are licensed under the AGPL-3.0 like the code, with the same freedoms and the same conditions. This page adds no limit to that license.
- **Trademark.** The name "Apprua" and the lighthouse logo are a brand. The rules above apply whenever they are used as a name or as a sign of where something comes from, whatever file they are taken from. So a changed version offered under another name should show its own name and logo, and not the Apprua lighthouse as its sign; the other pictures can stay.

Write to **brand@apprua.com**, or open an issue. We say yes to most reasonable requests.
